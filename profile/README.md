## 🎯 Mission

> **Give every storyteller a complete, open, and ethical AI toolkit** that handles the entire content pipeline — ideation, scripting, media generation, editing, publishing, and automation — so creators can focus on creativity instead of tooling.

We research and build practical applications specifically for **individual content creators** (YouTubers, educators, indie makers, technical creators). Our goal is a full ecosystem, not isolated demos.

---

## 🛠️ What We Build

| Domain | Focus | Flagship Projects |
|:------:|:------|:------------------|
| **🎞️ Media Generation & Editing** | Video, image, animation, captions | **[luffy-create](https://github.com/Creatorsai-Lab/luffy-create)** · content-claude-skills |
| **🤖 Agents** | Social media teams, voice, workflow automation | **[cupid](https://github.com/Creatorsai-Lab/cupid)** · neel-voice |
| **🧠 LLMs & Knowledge** | Content-specialized models & knowledge bases | Aura-LM · Content-Knowledge |

Everything is released under permissive licenses. Forks, issues, and PRs are welcome.

## 🌟 Why Creators AI Lab?

- **Creator-first** — Built for individuals who make content, not enterprise teams.
- **Full pipeline vision** — From idea → script → media → edit → publish → automate.
- **Open & ethical** — Transparent models, permissive licenses, no lock-in.
- **Practical over flashy** — Real applications you can run today (see `luffy-create`).
- **Composable** — Projects are designed to work together as an ecosystem.
