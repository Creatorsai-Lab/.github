## 🥁 Our Mission

> **Empower every storyteller** with open, ethical, and composable AI tooling  
> that amplifies creativity without locking you into walled gardens.

We focus on:

1. **Transparency** – open weights & reproducible research.  
2. **Accessibility** – low compute footprints, pastel-smooth DX.  
3. **Community-first** – collab channels & public roadmaps.
